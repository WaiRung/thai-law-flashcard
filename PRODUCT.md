# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Thai law students and exam-prep learners who need a reliable online reference while studying on phones and laptops.
Their core job is to quickly find, review, and remember law sections, then self-check understanding through flashcards, quizzes, diagrams, and source documents.

## Product Purpose
Thai Law provides structured, bilingual legal study support in one place so learners can move from lookup to understanding faster.
Success looks like short, repeatable study sessions where users can locate the right law content quickly, stay oriented, and retain concepts for exams and practical reference.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, calm, clear.
Voice should feel formal and academically grounded with a heritage-aware tone that respects legal context without feeling heavy or intimidating.

## Anti-references
Avoid neon visual styles and loud gamer aesthetics.
Avoid generic AI-looking UI patterns (overused gradients, template-like card sameness, decorative noise that weakens legal credibility).

## Design Principles
1. Trust through clarity: present legal information in clean, predictable structures that reduce ambiguity.
2. Study flow first: optimize every screen for quick scan, selection, and continuation of a study session.
3. Bilingual legibility: prioritize Thai-first readability while keeping English support contextual and unobtrusive.
4. Calm focus over spectacle: motion and color should guide attention, not compete with content.
5. Evidence-linked learning: keep pathways between flashcards, sections, diagrams, and source documents explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA baseline across the application.
Prioritize Thai-first readability with strong text contrast, clear hierarchy, and touch-friendly controls for mobile study contexts.
Respect reduced-motion preferences by keeping animations optional and non-blocking.
